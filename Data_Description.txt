A. AI & Technology (The Core Variables)
THNQ (ROBO Global Artificial Intelligence ETF): Tracks companies leading the AI revolution, specifically focusing on computing, data, and infrastructure.

ROBT (First Trust Nasdaq AI & Robotics ETF): Focuses on the "physical" side of AI—companies involved in robotics, automation, and the software that powers them.

SRVR (Pacer Benchmark Data & Infrastructure Real Estate ETF): Tracks the "physical home" of AI: the data centers and cell towers required to process and store AI algorithms.

B. Critical Minerals & Infrastructure
REMX (VanEck Rare Earth/Strategic Metals ETF): Represents the supply chain for AI hardware. Rare earth metals are essential for the high-end chips and magnets used in AI servers.

NCLR (VanEck Uranium+Nuclear Energy ETF): Tracks the nuclear energy sector, which is increasingly viewed as the "clean" baseload power source required to sustain 24/7 AI data centers.

C. Clean Energy & Sustainability (The ESG Cluster)
ICLN (iShares Global Clean Energy ETF): The primary benchmark for the renewable energy sector, including solar and wind power.

GRNB (VanEck Green Bond ETF): Tracks the performance of the "Green Bond" market—debt specifically issued to fund climate and environmental projects.

SUSA (iShares MSCI USA ESG Select ETF): A broad institutional benchmark of US companies with high Environmental, Social, and Governance ratings.

CRBN (iShares MSCI ACWI Low Carbon Target ETF): Specifically focuses on companies with a low carbon footprint, representing the "Decarbonization" trend.

DSI (iShares MSCI KLD 400 Social ETF): Represents the "Social and Governance" side of sustainability, excluding companies deemed unethical.

D. Macroeconomic Control Variables
WTI (West Texas Intermediate): The global benchmark for crude oil prices. In your study, this represents the "Dirty Energy" counter-balance to your clean energy variables.

GLD (SPDR Gold Shares): Tracks the price of gold bullion. It serves as your "Safe Haven" proxy to see if AI or Clean Energy assets behave like risky stocks or stable stores of value.

USD (US Dollar Index): Measures the strength of the US Dollar. It is a critical control for global liquidity and currency-driven price fluctuations.
